# Security

If you find a security problem with Tally, please tell us at **contact@milux.co.uk**. Put "Security" in the subject line, and describe what you found and how to see it. Please do not publish it until we have had a chance to fix it.

We will reply within five working days, tell you what we are doing about it, and credit you if you would like.

Tally has no accounts and sets no cookies. It has no bug bounty.

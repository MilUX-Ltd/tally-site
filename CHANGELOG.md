# Changelog

All notable changes to the Tally site. Versions follow semantic versioning.

## [Unreleased]

### Changed
- The Money view's lines to overseas countries are thinner: 1 to 4 pixels by value (were 1.5 to 10).
- The new companies feed (`/new/feed.xml`) carries only the newest 50 companies; the `/new` page still lists every company added in the 12 weeks.
- The capability matrix is a view of its own: the home page's view switch is Map, List and Matrix (`view=matrix`). The Matrix view is the whole width with no map: a card headed "Compare capabilities" for the current results, a map area or, with the shortlist open, the shortlist; the capability groups are a row of choices in place of a drop-down; the company column and the header rows stay in place while the table scrolls inside the card; each row has its own shortlist button. "Compare capabilities" in the results panel (beside the map, in the List view and on the shortlist) opens it, and the matrix no longer sits under the rows of the List view. A link with `mgroup` or `mpage` and no `view` now opens the Matrix view (it opened the List view). Choosing a view moves focus to that view's heading.
- Clusters and events (`/events`) redesigned (Tally redesign, slice C): the headline "Regional defence clusters." with a fact strip; a flat map of every cluster area drawn as SVG at build time from the committed ONS outlines, filled on the data ramp by upcoming events, with a pin counting each cluster's events, every cluster named beside the map (each name leads to its card), a legend and the same information as a table; a "Next up" timeline by day with large date numerals; a card for each cluster with its area, status, members recorded, upcoming events, next event, links and a link that opens Find on that cluster (`/?cluster=<id>`); then the calendar and the full list as before. Members-only events remain signposts wherever they appear. The page still runs no script.

### Added
- The Money view has a Measure choice: Contract awards (as before, the default) or MOD spend with industry (`mmeasure=spend`), MOD's official direct spend with UK industry by region from its annual statistics (Open Government Licence v3.0), read at build from `mod-spend.json` in the data repo. MOD spend shows the UK total, a Financial year choice of the last five years (`mfy=2025-26`; a year not in the data falls back to the latest), the map shaded by spend with no overseas lines, a table of the 12 regions with spend and spend per person, and "About these figures" with the source and its limits. Contract awards gains a line for scale with the official total and a button to switch. The build refuses a malformed `mod-spend.json`. Card 7475.
- Find, the full-map home page (Tally redesign, slice B): the map fills the window, in 3D columns by default (`flat=1` for flat hexagons), coloured on the data ramp against the busiest area in view; over it sit the headline, one search form (What, Near, Within), three example searches and a fact strip from the data. Any search, filter, place, area or shared link swaps the hero for a compact search bar, a chip for every active filter with Clear all, and a results panel (nearest first with distances when a place is set). A defence site typed in Near works like a place. Filters open in a side panel ending "Show n companies"; a Layers control holds clusters and defence sites; "Search this area" replaces "Use the map's current view"; the List view (`view=list`) holds the matrix and the overseas companies; the shortlist opens in the panel from the top bar, and a shared `list` link opens on it. The clusters panel, events and growth over time have left the home page; a `month` link shows a notice pointing to New companies. Wording follows the content review (rows 1 to 17).
- Redesign, slice C: About, company pages, Ask Tally and the 404 page in the Tally design language.
  - Company pages: the name as the headline, a mono location line, Add to shortlist and Website side by side, and the check line ("Last checked against public sources on <date>." or "From public sources. Not yet checked."); what they do, products, capabilities as tags by capability group without repeating the group's name (a group that is the company's one capability there shows it as the tag), and the record (accreditations with their labels as before, frameworks, memberships, founded and size) with "None recorded" throughout. "Where they are" is a locator drawn at build time with no map library: the company's point on a UK outline with its coordinates, its local area with a 25 mile ring and its three nearest defence sites; its region shaded when only the region is published; the country for an overseas company, with no "Region: Overseas". New shared files `/maps/uk-local.svg` and `/maps/region/<region>.svg`; `/maps/uk.svg` is redrawn from ONS regions and countries (`src/data/uk-regions.json`, built by hand with `scripts/build-uk-regions.mjs`; Open Government Licence v3.0). The back link reads "Back to search".
  - About: an editorial page with one tally mark for each company, "How Tally works" in three numbered steps, where the information comes from, how the search uses AI, "Data sources and licences" (`#licences`, holding the attribution wording for places, defence sites, cluster areas, map tiles and typefaces), get listed and correct a listing in a call-out, and how it was built.
  - Ask Tally: the same content with a contents column, numbered steps and mono code blocks, light on ink.
  - 404: a large "404.", "Page not found", what may have happened and "Search for a company".
- `/events.ics`: the upcoming cluster events as a calendar file, written at build. Events are all-day entries with the published time in the description; members-only events carry only the signpost line, the cluster and its public website.
- Funding (`/opportunities`) redesigned as a deadline board: each call is a row with the days left on a tile coloured by the data ramp, the closing date and a bar that fills over the weeks before the deadline, soonest first, with calls that have no closing date grouped after. Day counts are worked out in the browser from the dates, because a count made at build goes stale; without JavaScript the dates show. The hero carries the facts (open calls, closing soon, clusters, last updated) and the call that closes next; kind and cluster are filter chips, held in the URL as before; "Check with the funder before you apply" sits beside the list.
- New companies (`/new`) redesigned: the growth of the directory drawn at build time as the hexagon columns of the Find map, one per month, on the data ramp, with every capability group, a month slider, Play (not offered under reduced motion) and the figures as a table; a strip of how many companies were added in each of the 12 weeks; then the additions by week on a date rail, the latest week open and earlier weeks behind their headings, with a card for each company. Growth over time moves here from the home page, and `/new?month=YYYY-MM` opens on that month.
- Redesign foundation: the Tally design language in `base.css` (paper, ink and signal tokens, the data ramp, type, focus ring, buttons, fields, cards, tags, kicker, fact strip and page hero), Manrope and Roboto Mono self-hosted as WOFF2 (SIL OFL 1.1), and a shared top bar (`SiteHeader.astro`) and footer on every page through the base layout. The navigation is the same everywhere: Find, Clusters and events, Funding, New companies, About; on a phone it is a menu that works without JavaScript. Existing pages keep their layout on the new base.
- New companies (`/new`): companies added in the 12 weeks up to the latest addition, grouped by week, newest first, each with a link to its company page, its region, top capabilities and the date added, and an RSS 2.0 feed of the same list at `/new/feed.xml`, advertised in the page head. The added date is when a company first appeared on the data repo's `main`, read from its git history at build time. Linked from the explorer header and the footer, and listed in the sitemap.
- Walking skeleton: holding page, 404 page, security headers baseline, public-terms guard, CI with lint, type check, unit tests and the accessibility floor, preview and production deploy jobs.
- Capability heatmap: a full-width UK map (MapLibre, OpenFreeMap tiles) with companies binned into H3 hexagons in the browser, coloured by count with a brand legend, turning into clustered pins when zoomed in. Selecting a hexagon, cluster or pin lists its companies.
- Filters for capability (by group), size, region, framework, accreditation and membership, redrawing the map and the paged results list.
- Build step that fetches the public data and taxonomy and slims them for the browser; a synthetic test fixture so tests never use the network.
- Every view in the URL: filters, map centre and zoom, the selected hexagon or companies, the area, the matrix view and the results page are written to the query string and restored on load. Parameters are documented in `CONTEXT.md`.
- Capability matrix linked both ways with the map: companies against top-level capabilities in the ten groups, a page of rows at a time; ticking a column sets the capability filter and redraws the map; taking the map's current view or drawing a rectangle narrows the matrix. Fully keyboard operable.
- Shortlist: add or remove companies from the list or a map area; the shortlist is held in the URL (`list`), with a link to share, CSV download (columns in `CONTEXT.md`, formula-safe), a secondary "Book a call" link and the built-with-AI line.
- Capability overlap: with two or more capabilities chosen, a toggle switches from any of them to only the places where all of them are found (`capmatch=all`); the map, count, matrix and list follow.
- Overlap mode reads more clearly: the count says how many companies have a chosen capability and in how many places all overlap, never announces zero while loading, `capmatch` is left out of the URL with fewer than two capabilities, and a shortlist downloaded in overlap mode is named `tally-shortlist-overlap.csv`.
- Overseas companies listed by country, Europe first then the rest of the world, following the same filters; an explicit guard in the build and the browser keeps overseas and out-of-bounds points off the UK map.
- Growth over time: a slider (with play) replays the directory's size by month, overall and per capability group, from a small file built at build time from the data repo's git history; the month is held in the URL (`month`).
- Defence sites overlay: 53 widely published UK defence sites, each verified against its Wikipedia article (CC BY-SA 4.0, attributed), can be drawn on the map; choosing a site and a radius (10, 25 or 50 miles) draws a ring and narrows the map, matrix and list to companies within it. Held in the URL (`site`, `radius`, `sites`).
- Cluster regions overlay: the areas of the 16 UK regional defence and security clusters, merged and simplified from ONS Open Geography boundaries (Open Government Licence v3.0, attributed), drawn in each cluster's colour with a marker counting its upcoming events. Choosing a cluster narrows the companies to its recorded members (`mbr-` tag) and lists its upcoming events. Held in the URL (`clusters`, `cluster`).
- Funding calls and opportunities (`/opportunities`): open funding calls and opportunities shared by the clusters, soonest deadline first, each with its deadline, cluster, summary and public link; filter by cluster and kind, held in the URL (`cluster`, `kind`). Linked from the explorer header and the events page. Members-only items are never published; the build refuses any field outside the contract.
- Cluster map: a "Cluster map" button above the map (the same switch as the Clusters panel checkbox, `clusters=1`) draws all 16 cluster areas with name labels in a colourblind-aware map palette (the clusters' house colours stay as data; clusters not yet live are a dashed outline), fades the companies behind them (`ontop=1` brings them back) and leaves out the event markers, and shows a legend of every cluster with its Tally members (where recorded) and upcoming events. Choosing a legend entry or selecting an area chooses that cluster; smaller areas, such as Greater Lincolnshire, draw on top where areas overlap.
- Certification labels on company pages: each accreditation says where it came from, "Stated by the company" by default or "Checked against <register> on <date>" when the data repo records a manual check (`certification_checks`). The build keeps only well-formed checks for accreditations the company carries, in a build-only file; nothing ever says that a company is not certified. A company with no accreditations shows "None recorded in Tally".
- `/events`: upcoming cluster events as a month calendar and a list, built at build time; past events are dropped. Members-only events show only a signpost and a link to join the cluster, and the build refuses a members-only event that carries anything more.
- Bundle-size check in the build, and journey tests run under the production CSP.
- Plain-English search: a search box sends the request to `POST /api/search`, a Worker route on the same site that asks Claude Haiku for filter values through one strict tool, validates them against the filter schema and the known values, and returns filters only. The route is rate-limited and budget-capped; without a key, at a limit, or on any failure it answers `{"fallback": true}` and the browser runs a keyword search over names, summaries and tag labels, held in the URL (`q`), and says so. Contract in `CONTEXT.md`.
- A page for each company at `/companies/<id>/`, built statically: summary, what they do, products, capabilities grouped by capability group, size, region, location with a small static map (a shared UK outline from the committed ONS boundaries and a marker; none for region-level, overseas or unplaced companies), frameworks, accreditations, memberships, website and LinkedIn links, the date the public sources were verified, Add to shortlist (keeping the shortlist the visitor came with) and links back to the explorer and the map. Company text is safe Markdown: paragraphs, lists, emphasis and http(s) links only. Company names in the results, map area, overseas lists and matrix link to their pages.
- `/sitemap.xml` and a `Sitemap` line in `robots.txt`; every page has a canonical URL on tally.milux.co.uk, and company pages have their own titles and descriptions.
- "Correct this listing" on every company page and a site-wide "Get listed" link (footer and explorer header): plain mail links to contact@milux.co.uk with a pre-filled subject and prompt. No form, nothing stored, no third party.
- `/about`: what Tally is, public sources only, licences and credits, built with AI, and how to get listed or correct a listing. Every page shares one footer linking to it.
- Near-me search: type a UK postcode, the first part of one, or a town or city, and pick 5, 10, 25 or 50 miles; a ring is drawn and the map, count, matrix and list narrow to companies within it. Places are looked up with postcodes.io from the browser, only on Find, sending only the typed place. The resolved place is held in the URL (`near`, `at`, `within`). One location filter at a time: a place and a defence site replace each other. Combines with filters, clusters and overlap mode.

- Ask Tally: a read-only remote MCP server at `/mcp` on the same Worker, so anyone can connect an AI assistant to Tally. Four tools (`search_companies`, `get_company`, `list_capabilities`, `list_clusters_and_events`) answer from the site's own public data with strict input schemas, at most 25 results, generic errors that never repeat the request, no batches, foreign browser origins refused, a strict rate limit per connection and per address, and no model calls. A new `/ask-tally` page explains how to add it in Claude and other MCP clients. Contract in `CONTEXT.md`.

### Changed
- CSP allows the place lookup (`api.postcodes.io`) for connect only.
- Region-level companies are refused by the browser's `isLocated` guard too, so they are never mapped or inside any radius.
- Pull-request previews use Worker Previews (`wrangler preview`), since a Worker that implements a Durable Object gets no version preview URL; the deploy check also exercises the search route.
- The deploy check also calls `/mcp` with an MCP initialize and a tools list.
- CSP allows the map tile host (`tiles.openfreemap.org`) for connect and images only.
